About

Who is Longa?

I care about my work, but who I am goes beyond what I do. Integrity, empathy and continuous learning guide me.

Outside engineering I spend time with family and friends, travel to learn about new cultures, and keep up with art, music and technology. I'm happiest outdoors or experimenting with digital design.

The engineer

I enjoy solving complex problems across electronics, PCB design, CAD and control, and turning abstract ideas into working hardware.

The artist

For me, art is about expression and joy. Paint and pencil let me capture fleeting moments and ideas, and each piece reflects how I see the world.

The coder

Code is where creativity meets technology. Today that mostly means deep learning and computer vision, plus the occasional website like this one.
